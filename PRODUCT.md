# Billy Ray Taylor — Speaker Site

register: brand

## Product purpose
Standalone one-page speaker site for Billy Ray Taylor: global keynote speaker, author of The Winning Link, founder of LinkedXL, former North American Director at Goodyear. The single job of the page is to get corporate event decision-makers to book him. Rebuilt from https://linkedxl.com/billyraytaylor/ with https://cassandraworthy.com/ as the taste reference.

## Users
Corporate event planners, HR/L&D leaders, and executives at major companies (manufacturing, supply chain, operations-heavy Fortune 500s) evaluating keynote speakers for conferences, kickoffs, and leadership summits. They skim on a laptop between meetings and decide in about 90 seconds whether a speaker has mainstage energy and enterprise credibility.

## Brand voice
Loud, warm, engineered. Billy's message is that winning is a repeatable system: define, align, execute, make the score visible. The site should feel like a varsity banner drawn by an operations engineer: athletic conviction with operational rigor underneath.

## Tone rules
- Every number and claim comes from Billy's own published page. Never invent stats, clients, or credentials.
- Goodyear result is always phrased as "helped take / helped drive" (his page says "contributing to").
- Fun and high-energy, but enterprise-credible. He speaks to Nike, GE, and the U.S. Army.

## Anti-references
- Navy-and-gold leadership-guru template sites.
- Editorial-magazine serif aesthetics (wrong lane for a factory-floor operator).
- Generic AI landing pages: hero scroll cues, image pills in headlines, status pills with green dots, glassmorphism.

## Strategic principles
- Cassandra Worthy reference: branded IP up front, huge display type, pull-quote testimonial headlines, repeated booking CTAs, personality-led storytelling.
- Billy's IP: "How We Win" keynote, The Winning Link book, the 3 Deliberates (clarity, ownership, practice), LinkedXL process, EXPRESSED / The Morning Tea with Billy T podcast.
- All CTAs resolve to real destinations: Drive downloads, Amazon, YouTube, tel:, mailto:.
