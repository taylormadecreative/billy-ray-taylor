Meet Billy Ray Taylor

Billy Ray Taylor is a global keynote speaker, the author of The Winning Link, and the founder of LinkedXL, a global consulting and software company that empowers organizations to win through strategic implementation of the Connected Business Model.

Before launching LinkedXL, Billy was the North American Director of Goodyear Tire, leading multiple turnarounds and contributing to a dramatic change in the company's earnings, from a $38 million loss to over $1 billion. That experience became the LinkedXL process, a proven system for building business operations that win.

Today he empowers leaders and organizations to run that same playbook, achieving record-breaking results through keynotes, consulting, thought leadership, and software.
